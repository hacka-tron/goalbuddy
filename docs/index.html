Core Modes

Three ways to crush your goals

Whether you want a personal cheerleader, a co-pilot, or just the right match — GoalBuddy has a mode for you.

Buddy Mode

You and a partner each chase your own goal in the same category. You coach each other, share stats, and keep each other honest — without being on the exact same path.

Co-op Mode

One person publishes a goal with milestones, the other joins and gets a copy. You both tackle the same objective and celebrate the same wins.

Goal Finder

Browse public goals or swipe through a smart deck of recommended partners. Get matched by category, commitment level, and schedule — then connect in seconds.
