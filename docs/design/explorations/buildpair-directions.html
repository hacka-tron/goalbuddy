Buildpair, three ways

Buildpair helps founders set real goals and get honest check-ins from peers who aren't too close to their work. Each direction below draws the same two screens with the same example data.

  • Your week: your season goal, this week's check-in, and your partner's outside view in reply.
  • Getting started: connect Jira, pick the epics that matter this season, and they become your goals. Or add goals by hand.

Getting started: Jira import

Feels like
A thoughtful editor or mentor reading your work: personal, honest, calm.
Why it fits
The core idea, an outside view, becomes the layout itself. Your update is the page, and your partners' feedback is in the margin.
Signature
Margin notes in royal blue, with structured prompts (going well, I'd focus on, honest take) that make candid feedback easy to give.
Risk
Long threads could get cramped on small phones, so replies need a clean "see all" view.

Feels like
Subway signage and Swiss posters: bold, graphic, memorable.
Why it fits
Goals as destinations is easy to grasp for any founder, and the route makes progress visible at a glance.
Signature
The season route, with everyone's lines heading to their goal.
Risk
The hardest to build well, especially with 4–5 lines on a phone. The feedback (the core idea) gets less space than the map.

What I need from you

Pick a direction, or a mix such as "Margin Notes with the Week Ledger". I'll then build the full design system in it: colors, type, spacing, components, and the iPhone, Android and web rules, plus the theme file the app will use.

Earlier versions of this page also showed a sporty "Training Partner" direction and a generic startup look. I dropped both because they pull toward the gamified or template feel you want to avoid.
