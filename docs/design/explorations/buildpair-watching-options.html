A. One chat per goal

Like a messaging app: every shared goal is its own conversation, newest activity on top.

  • Each row shows the latest event and a thin progress line.
  • Group chats sit in the same list.
  • Notifications live behind the bell.
  • Risk: someone sharing 4 goals fills your list.

B. One chat per person

Each person (or group) gets a single row. Tap in to see all the goals they share with you, each as its own thread.

  • Feels like keeping up with friends, not tracking goals.
  • Scales well when people share many goals.
  • Risk: one extra tap to reach a specific goal.

Notifications: the Activity screen

Everything that happened to you: reactions, replies, accepted requests, reminders. Opened from the bell at the top of Today and Watching.

  • New items are lightly tinted until seen.
  • Tapping an item opens the right thread.
  • The same items arrive as phone notifications, which you can tune in settings.

Where notifications could live

A bell at the top of Today and Watching (drawn here) keeps four tabs and stays quiet. The alternative is a fifth Activity tab, which is more visible but adds another tab. I recommend the bell, because the "Check in on" strip already surfaces anything urgent.
